TradingSignalBot

Open-source signal tools

TradingView strategy indicators, a kit to run your own Telegram signal channel, a webhook bridge, a record auditor and a chart renderer. Free, with no account and no key, from the team behind the TradingSignalBot Telegram bot.

TradingView

Strategy indicator pack

Pine Script v6 indicators for the London breakout, opening range breakout, break and retest, Supertrend, EMA and MACD crossovers, RSI divergence and the liquidity sweep. Each draws its levels, the entry, the stop and the targets, with alerts.

GitHubThe strategies
Telegram

Signal channel starter kit

A Telegram bot that posts a signal from a command or a TradingView alert and keeps a pinned record that counts losses, with risk-disclosure and channel-rules templates.

GitHubThe guide
Self-hosted

TradingView webhook bridge

Takes TradingView alert webhooks and forwards them to Telegram, Discord or an MT5 expert advisor, behind a shared secret. One Docker image; Railway and Cloudflare Workers too.
